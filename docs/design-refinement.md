# ControlWard refinement handoff

## Pair and sequence

Impeccable → Diamond Eyes. Skill Pairing coordinates the handoff.

## Impeccable: diagnosis and implementation handoff

Surface: native product UI, especially the overview, navigation, review inbox,
saved views and pipeline. Evidence: synthetic native screenshots and accessibility
trees, Theme.swift and the working UI components.

Findings: the six metric cards competed with the work; repeated sidebar branding
hid navigation; small amber text had insufficient contrast on light backgrounds;
several section headings were uppercase; opportunity stages compressed into six
narrow columns; saved-view buttons needed explicit accessible labels.

Implementation: action-led overview, compact sidebar, darker action amber,
consistent headline hierarchy, Reduce Motion support, native keyboard actions,
scrollable opportunity lanes, labelled saved-view actions. Preserve all source
and project authority boundaries. Do not restyle or authenticate the hosted
Email Aggregator service.

## Diamond Eyes: final cut

Mode: Whisper Cut, with Charged Precision at the capture decision.
Live edge: the PHAROS mark, amber/ink identity and the owner's project decisions.
Preserve those. Cut duplicate branding and the command-center thesis. Sharpen
labels into actions: Open projects, Next actions, Ready to review. Keep connection
limits explicit. Remove decorative status dots and use counts or actual states.

The result is an implemented workspace, not a new branding concept. Verification
and release details are in controlward.md and the accompanying evidence report.
